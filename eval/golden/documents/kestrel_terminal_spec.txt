KESTREL TERMINAL
AUTOMATED CARGO HANDLING SYSTEM
TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION — REVISION D

Document number: KT-SPEC-0004-D
Issued: 2 October 2024
Supersedes: KT-SPEC-0004-C dated 19 April 2024
Classification: Unrestricted

1. SCOPE

This specification defines the functional and performance requirements for the automated
cargo handling system to be installed at Kestrel Terminal. It covers automated stacking
cranes, ship-to-shore gantry cranes, the terminal operating system interface, and the
electrical supply and backup provisions required to support them.

This specification does not cover civil works, quay wall construction, dredging, or the
landside gate complex, which are procured separately.

2. EQUIPMENT SCHEDULE

2.1 Ship-to-shore gantry cranes
    Quantity: 12 units
    Model: Orbis Meridian-class STS
    Outreach: 68 meters
    Rated lifting capacity: 65 tonnes under spreader
    Rated hoist speed, loaded: 90 meters per minute
    Rated hoist speed, empty: 180 meters per minute

2.2 Automated stacking cranes
    Quantity: 44 units
    Model: Orbis ASC-9
    Stacking height: 1 over 5 containers
    Rail gauge: 31 meters
    Positioning tolerance: plus or minus 15 millimeters

2.3 Automated guided vehicles
    Quantity: 96 units
    Battery chemistry: lithium iron phosphate
    Nominal charge cycle: 90 minutes to 80 percent

3. PERFORMANCE REQUIREMENTS

3.1 The system shall sustain a berth productivity of not less than 38 container moves per
crane per hour, averaged over any continuous eight-hour period.

3.2 The system shall achieve a terminal annual throughput of 1.8 million TEU at a stacking
density not exceeding 1,450 TEU per hectare.

3.3 System availability shall be not less than 99.2 percent, measured monthly and excluding
scheduled maintenance windows agreed under section 7.

3.4 Mean time to recover from a single-crane fault shall not exceed 45 minutes.

4. ELECTRICAL SUPPLY

4.1 Total connected load for the automated cargo handling system is 41.5 megawatts.

4.2 Peak simultaneous demand, applying a diversity factor of 0.62, is 25.7 megawatts.

4.3 Supply shall be taken at 33 kilovolts from two independent feeders originating at
separate substations. Loss of either feeder shall not reduce system availability below the
threshold in section 3.3.

4.4 Regenerative braking energy recovered from hoist lowering shall be returned to the
terminal distribution network. The supplier shall demonstrate a minimum recovery of 22
percent of hoist energy consumption.

4.5 Backup generation sized at 8 megawatts shall support safe container set-down, crane
parking, and terminal operating system continuity for a minimum of four hours following
total loss of utility supply.

5. SUPPLIER OBLIGATIONS

5.1 The supplier of record for all equipment in section 2 is Orbis Robotics, appointed under
the automation contract approved by the Meridian Harbor Authority Board on 14 June 2024.

5.2 The supplier shall maintain a resident engineering team of not fewer than six qualified
personnel on site from the commencement of crane erection until twelve months after
provisional acceptance.

5.3 The supplier shall deliver operator and maintenance training for not fewer than 180
Authority and terminal-operator personnel prior to provisional acceptance. Training content
shall be developed in consultation with the recognized labor representative.

6. ACCEPTANCE

6.1 Provisional acceptance requires demonstration of the performance requirements in
section 3 over a continuous 30-day proving period.

6.2 Final acceptance occurs twelve months after provisional acceptance, subject to no
unresolved category-A defects.

6.3 Scheduled commissioning of the terminal is Q3 2027. Liquidated damages accrue at 140,000
dollars per week of supplier-attributable delay beyond that date, capped at 8 percent of
contract value.

7. MAINTENANCE WINDOWS

7.1 Scheduled maintenance shall be confined to windows agreed not less than 30 days in
advance and shall not exceed 96 hours in any calendar month.

7.2 Software updates to the terminal operating system interface shall be applied only within
agreed windows and shall be reversible to the prior release within two hours.
