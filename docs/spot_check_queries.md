# Spot-Check Evaluation Queries

This list contains 15 representative queries to manually verify RAG answer quality, retrieval consistency, and citation accuracy.

## Simple Factual
1. Who is the primary instructor for the course?
2. What is the grading breakdown?
3. What textbook is used?
4. When is the final exam?
5. Is attendance mandatory?
6. Where is the instructor's office located?

## Multi-Hop / Complex
7. How do homework assignments impact the final grade, and when is the first one due?
8. What is the policy for late submissions, and who do I contact for extensions?
9. Explain the collaboration policy and what constitutes plagiarism in this course.

## Ambiguous / Conversational
10. How does the grade work?
11. What about homework?
12. Is there any prerequisite?
13. Tell me about the exams.

## Out-of-Corpus / General Knowledge
14. Who won the World Cup in 2022?
15. What is the capital of France?
